# 1v1 LOL - Battle Arena 🎮⚔️

A web-based 1v1 PvP battle arena game inspired by 1v1.LOL. Fight opponents in real-time with shooting, melee combat, and strategic gameplay.

## Features

✅ **Real-time PvP Combat** - Battle against another player on the same device
🎯 **Shooting Mechanics** - Click to shoot bullets with ammo management
⚔️ **Melee Attacks** - Press E (P1) or R (P2) for close-range combat
🏃 **Movement & Jumping** - Full 2D movement with gravity and jumping
❤️ **Health System** - Track player health with visual health bars
🤖 **AI Opponent** - Play against computer (coming soon)
👥 **Character Selection** - Choose your character (coming soon)

## How to Play

### Controls

**Player 1 (Green):**
- **WASD** - Move left/right
- **SPACE** - Jump
- **CLICK** - Shoot (uses ammo)
- **E** - Melee attack

**Player 2 (Pink):**
- **Arrow Keys** - Move left/right
- **UP Arrow** - Jump
- **SHIFT** - Shoot (uses ammo)
- **R** - Melee attack

### Objective
Reduce your opponent's health to 0 to win!

## Game Mechanics

- **Health**: Each player starts with 100 HP
- **Ammo**: 30 bullets per magazine, regenerates over time
- **Bullets**: Deal 10 damage on hit
- **Melee**: Deal 15 damage if within range
- **Invincibility**: Brief invincibility after taking damage
- **Gravity**: Realistic physics with jumping and falling

## Installation & Running

1. Clone this repository
2. Open `index.html` in your web browser
3. Start playing!
